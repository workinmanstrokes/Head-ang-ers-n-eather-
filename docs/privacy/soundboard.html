DowntownDiddlerrr · Laugh Pad: Meme Soundboard

Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · Android package com.diddlerrr.laughpad

Laugh Pad: Meme Soundboard is a soundboard made by DowntownDiddlerrr. On Android it is installed under the name Laugh Pad, package com.diddlerrr.laughpad. You tap a pad and a short sound plays. This page says exactly what the app does with information. It does not show ads and it does not use an account.

What the app does

  • Plays a built-in pack of original synthesized sound effects. Those sounds are part of the app. They are not recordings of people.
  • Lets you record your own sound with the microphone and place it on a pad, if you choose to.
  • Lets you search the Internet Archive for short audio that has a clear free license (public domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA), preview it, and save a copy onto a pad.
  • Lets you rename, delete, or download a pad, and chain up to eight pads into one tap.
  • Plays sound through this device’s speaker.
  • Can send anonymous crash reports and play counts, unless you turn that switch off.

Microphone

The microphone is used only after you open Record and tap Record. It is not used at launch, and it is not used for the built-in pads. The recording is kept in the app’s private storage on this device. Laugh Pad: Meme Soundboard does not upload, share, or send recordings to DowntownDiddlerrr or to anyone else. If you deny microphone access, the app tells you and the rest of the board keeps working. You can record again later if you allow the microphone in system settings.

On Android the app declares internet access (sound search, opening this page in the browser, and the optional crash reports and play stats below), the microphone, and the ordinary audio-settings permission the system WebView asks for at the same time as the microphone so a recording can start. The system asks you before the microphone is used. No other permissions are declared.

Internet and sound search

The built-in sounds play with no network. When you search, the words you type are sent over HTTPS to the Internet Archive at archive.org so it can return results. If you preview or save a result, the app downloads that audio file from archive.org. A saved file is then stored on this device. DowntownDiddlerrr does not receive your search words, the files, or a copy of what you play. Those search words are not sent to the crash or stats tools. The Internet Archive’s own policy applies to the request they receive.

Results without a public-domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA license are not shown and are not saved. When a clip is saved, the app keeps the license name and attribution with the pad.

The in-app Privacy link opens this page in the browser. That request is between your device and the site that hosts this page. DowntownDiddlerrr does not add tracking parameters to it.

On-device storage

Pad names, colours, chains, the volume setting, recordings, audio you save, and the crash-and-stats switch are stored only on the device (local storage and IndexedDB in the app’s private data). They are not synced to a DowntownDiddlerrr server. There is no account.

Crash reports and play stats

Laugh Pad: Meme Soundboard does not show ads. It does not include advertising SDKs. A build can include two optional tools so DowntownDiddlerrr can fix crashes and see that the app is being used. Both are free tiers. If the build was made without their keys, the app sends nothing and the rest of this section does not apply.

The switch is in More, labelled “Crash reports and play stats”. It is on until you turn it off, and the choice is saved on this device. Off means nothing in this section is sent. The app also stays quiet when the device sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, and when the page is opened from a local http server.

Crash reports (Sentry, browser SDK). If the app hits an error while the switch is on, a report may be sent with the error text and a short trail of what the app was doing. Reports do not include your name, an account, a user id, or an IP address stored on the event (the event’s IP field is cleared before it is sent). Text that looks like an email address is removed. Taps in text fields, and anything you type, are not recorded. Recordings, pad names, and search words are not attached.

Play stats (PostHog). Stats go to the shared PostHog project workinmanstrokesinc. Every event is labeled app = Laugh Pad. The app counts that it was opened, that a pad was played (only whether it was a built-in sound, a recording, or a saved clip), and that a chain was played. It does not record the screen. Session recording is off. Autocapture of taps and typing is off. Page addresses are not collected as a visit log. You are not identified. PostHog is configured with person profiles set to never, so these stats are not attached to a person profile. An anonymous id for those stats may be stored on this device only. It is not the Android advertising id. Turning the switch off stops new stats and clears that id on the device. The client is also configured not to attach an IP address to the event.

The connection to Sentry or PostHog still has to cross the network. DowntownDiddlerrr does not use either tool for advertising, and does not sell this information. A build with the switch on and with keys present is the Play build. A build with blank keys, including a debug build made without those keys, sends neither reports nor stats.

What we do not collect

DowntownDiddlerrr does not collect your name, contacts, photos, location, or the contents of your recordings. The app does not ask you to sign in. Search words stay between your device and the Internet Archive.

Deletion

Reset board, inside More, deletes the pads, recordings, saved clips, and chains stored by the app on this device and restores the original starter sounds. It does not by itself change the crash-and-stats switch. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s storage, removes the private storage, the switch, and the anonymous stats id. Crash reports are not tied to a person, so there is no profile to look up and delete. New reports and stats stop when the switch is off.

Children

Laugh Pad: Meme Soundboard is not directed at children under 13, and DowntownDiddlerrr does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional crash reports and play stats are anonymous, can be turned off, and are not a reason to enrol the app in a children’s store program.
